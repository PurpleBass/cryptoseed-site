CryptoSeed

Local, user-controlled cryptography for your iPhone.

Encrypt and sign text and files, manage keys and identities, generate two-factor codes, and exchange keys over NFC, QR or a direct nearby transfer. Everything runs on your device with Apple CryptoKit, with no servers, no accounts and no analytics.

Coming soon to the App Store

Features

  • Encryption for text and files with symmetric keys and X25519 identities, plus post-quantum X-Wing on iOS 26.
  • Digital signatures with Ed25519 and ML-DSA-65.
  • Trusted contacts and public-key verification via QR codes and NFC tags.
  • Authenticator for offline TOTP / HOTP two-factor codes.
  • Private Transfer: direct, encrypted, nearby device-to-device exchange.
  • Shortcuts actions for automation.
